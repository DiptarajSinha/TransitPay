# RULES.md

## Rules

1. **Use Riverpod ONLY (no Provider, no GetX)**
2. **Follow Feature-first architecture strictly**
3. **No business logic inside UI**
4. **All data access must go through repositories**
5. **Must support offline-first behavior**
6. **Always cache tickets locally**
7. **Realtime subscriptions must be disposed properly**
8. **No Firebase usage anywhere**
9. **Use Supabase only**
10. **All local storage must be encrypted**
11. **Code must be null-safe and production-ready**

## Important

These rules are mandatory and cannot be violated.
